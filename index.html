About us

A boutique advisory practice

Belgravia Advisor was founded by Leonardo Shinohara, who brings close to two decades of experience in investor relations, treasury, M&A and equity research across major listed companies including Embraer, CSN, AES Tietê and HSBC.

His track record spans IPO preparation, multi-billion-dollar capital raises, project finance structuring and post-listing investor relations turnarounds - giving clients direct access to capital markets and transaction expertise usually reserved for large institutions.

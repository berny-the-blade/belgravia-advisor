CONNECT belgraviaadvisor.com TO YOUR WEBSITE
=============================================

Your website is already built and live at a temporary Google address.
These steps point your real domain (belgraviaadvisor.com) at it, so
visitors can just type belgraviaadvisor.com and reach the site.

Takes about 10-15 minutes to do. Can take up to 48 hours to fully work
everywhere after you finish (usually much faster).


PART 1 — Add two records at Squarespace (where your domain lives)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Note: Google Domains sold its domain business to Squarespace in 2023,
so any domain you bought "through Google" now lives at Squarespace
Domains. You sign in with the same Google account you used to buy it.

1. Go to: domains.squarespace.com
2. Sign in with the Google account you used to buy belgraviaadvisor.com
3. Click on "belgraviaadvisor.com" in your domain list
4. Find "DNS Settings" (sometimes under an "Advanced" or "DNS" tab)

5. Add this record:
   Type:  CNAME
   Host:  www
   Value: ghs.googlehosted.com
   Save it.

6. Also set up domain forwarding (may be a separate "Domain Forwarding"
   section on the same page):
   Forward from:  belgraviaadvisor.com  (no "www")
   Forward to:    https://www.belgraviaadvisor.com
   Type:          Permanent (301)
   Save it.

That's Part 1 done.


PART 2 — Connect it in the Google Admin console
-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to: admin.google.com
2. Sign in with your Workspace admin account (same login as your Gmail)
3. In the left-hand menu: click "Domains" then "Manage domains"
4. Click on "belgraviaadvisor.com" in the list
5. Look for an option to connect a custom URL / website to this domain
   (wording can vary slightly) — select it, then choose the
   "Belgravia Advisor" site you already published in Google Sites
6. Confirm. Google will check for the CNAME record from Part 1.
   - If it says it can't find the record yet, wait 15-30 minutes
     (DNS takes a little time to update) and try the confirm step again.
7. It will show as "Pending" — that's normal, just leave it.

If any menu name doesn't match exactly what you see (Google updates
its layout sometimes), use the search bar at the top of admin.google.com
and type "domains" — it'll take you to the right place. Google's own
help page for this exact task is here if needed:
https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/domains/connect-your-website-to-a-domain-registered-through-google


WHAT TO EXPECT AFTER
-------------------------------------------------------------------
- 30-60 minutes: Google starts recognizing the domain
- Up to 48 hours: fully working everywhere in the world (usually faster)
- Once live: belgraviaadvisor.com will show the site automatically with
  a secure padlock (https) — nothing extra to set up for that part.

No further action needed after these two parts — it connects itself
once the DNS record is found.
